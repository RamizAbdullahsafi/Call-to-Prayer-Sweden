Disclaimer and information

Last updated: 10 April 2026.

1. Prayer times

Times are shown from the Islamic Association of Sweden prayer-times widget (or equivalent technical intermediary). Small differences may occur due to calculation method, updates, or technical errors.

For fasting and religious decisions, follow your local community or mosque and official announcements.

2. Friday prayer / Jumu’ah

If the app shows a Friday row, the time is based on the same source as that day’s Dhuhr; actual congregation time may differ between mosques.

3. Ramadan (imsak / iftar)

In Ramadan, Fajr and Maghrib are shown as practical references for starting and breaking the fast; local rules and mosque guidance may differ.

4. Qibla (compass and GPS)

The qibla angle is calculated geographically from your position when you use location. If you grant permission, the app may use the device compass or orientation sensor to show direction on screen. Accuracy varies by device, magnetic interference, and calibration — verify against a reliable source if needed.

5. Hijri calendar

Hijri dates follow common UI practice (Umm al-Qura). Local moon-sighting decisions may differ.

6. Location and city

GPS and place names are used to show estimated times. Wrong position or misspelled city may give wrong results.

7. No professional advice

The app is an information tool and does not provide legal, medical, or religious advice.
