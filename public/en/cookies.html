Cookies and Local Storage

Last updated: March 29, 2026.

1. Overview

Call to Prayer Sweden primarily uses local storage in the browser (e.g., localStorage) to save settings such as city, language, theme, and choices for reminders/adhan. This is necessary for the app to function as expected on your device.

2. Tracking and Marketing

The app is not built to sell advertisements or profile you for marketing. We do not use analytics cookies for behavioral tracking for commercial purposes.

3. Third Parties

When you choose features that fetch data or audio from external hosts, these may technically set cookies or store data according to the provider's policy. This applies, for example, to the playback of audio from specified sources.

4. Your Choices

You can clear website data in your browser settings; this will remove saved settings in the app. You can also turn off location/notification permissions in the device's system settings.
