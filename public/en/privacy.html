Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026.

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes how Call to Prayer Sweden handles information in the web app and related clients (PWA). We primarily process technical data needed to display prayer times and your chosen settings.

2. Personal Data

The app does not require an account. If you enter a city, date, or activate location features, such information is sent in requests to our serverless intermediary (Netlify Functions), which forwards it to the Islamic Association of Sweden's (IFIS) prayer times widget. The data is only used to return times to your device.

If you activate notifications, the browser or operating system may process technical identifiers according to the platform's own rules.

3. Local Storage

Settings (e.g., city, language, theme, adhan choices, reminders) are stored locally on your device via browser storage. Data is not sold and is not used to create advertising profiles in the app.

4. Audio and External Sources

When playing adhan, audio may be fetched from external hosts (e.g., AlAdhan, Wikimedia Commons). These may log technical connection data according to their own policies.

5. Location (GPS)

If you use "my position," the device's coordinates may be used to estimate the city and qibla. Processing occurs on the device and in requests that you trigger.

6. Legal Basis and Retention

For users in the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies where relevant. Local storage remains until you clear website data or uninstall the app. Server logs at the hosting provider may occur according to their standards (e.g., technical troubleshooting and security).

7. Your Rights

You may request information about processing, correction, or deletion to the extent applicable. Contact us via the project's public channel or the contact method in the app store.

8. Changes

The policy may be updated. The date of the latest version is shown at the top of the page.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: use contact details in the app store or the project's GitHub repository.
