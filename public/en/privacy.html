Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 April 2026.

This policy is intended to support compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Swedish practice (e.g. guidance from the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY), and requirements that follow from distribution via Google Play. It should reflect how the app actually works; where it differs, this text governs for users in the EU/EEA.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies to Call to Prayer Sweden in the browser, as an installed web app (PWA), and as the Android app built on the same content (Capacitor WebView). It describes what personal data may be processed, for what purpose, on what legal basis, and for how long.

2. Data controller

The controller is the developer of the app (RASafi Tech, as stated in the app). Contact is primarily via the contact details published in Google Play Console (developer contact for the app) and the public source repository: GitHub.

3. What data is processed?

  • City and date you enter (or that follow from your choices) are sent in requests to our serverless layer (Netlify Functions), which forwards them to the Islamic Association of Sweden (IF) to retrieve published prayer times. The data is used only to return times to your device.
  • Location (GPS) is used only if you actively request it (e.g. "my position"). Coordinates are processed on the device for qibla and may be used in a request you trigger to OpenStreetMap Nominatim for reverse geocoding (city name). We do not sell location data or build ad profiles in the app.
  • Notifications and alarms: If you enable reminders or adhan, the operating system and (on Android) the app’s scheduling (e.g. local notifications, exact alarms, background work per permissions you approve) apply. Processing follows the platform provider’s (Google/Android) terms and privacy policy where they act as controller.
  • Settings and cache (city, language, theme, adhan choice, reminders, etc.) are stored locally (browser storage and/or Capacitor Preferences). Prayer times may also be cached locally for temporary offline use.
  • Adhan audio is delivered as files bundled in the app. Playback normally does not require fetching audio from external hosts.
  • Technical logs may be created at our hosting provider (Netlify) when the API is called (e.g. time, IP address, error data) for operations, security, and troubleshooting under the provider’s standard practices.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

Processing necessary to provide the service you use (show prayer times, your settings) is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract / steps at your request). The app does not normally process special categories of personal data under Article 9.

For location services and notifications on the device, your consent or equivalent platform approval also applies where required by applicable law (GDPR / ePrivacy and the OS model).

5. Recipients and processors

Data may be shared with: Netlify (hosting for the function that fetches prayer times), IF (source of published times), OpenStreetMap Nominatim (when you trigger reverse geocoding), and Google as OS and Play store provider. We do not use our own marketing or analytics cookies to track you across websites.

6. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some processing may occur outside the EU/EEA (e.g. USA with Netlify or Google). Such transfers are carried out in accordance with GDPR, including the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or other approved mechanisms, to the extent the provider commits to them.

7. Retention

Local settings and cache remain until you clear app/browser data or uninstall. Server logs at providers are retained under their policies (often limited technical retention).

8. Your rights

You have rights to information, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability under GDPR. You may lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se, or your local supervisory authority.

9. Google Play and Android

When you install via Google Play, Google’s terms and privacy policy also apply where relevant. The app may request permissions such as notifications, exact alarms, location, foreground playback, and battery optimisation exemptions depending on version — to deliver prayer times, qibla, and reminders/adhan. Information declared in Play’s Data safety form should match this policy.

10. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided data, contact us via Play or GitHub and we will help delete it where feasible.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated; material changes should be reflected in the date above and in the store listing where required.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact in Google Play or open an issue on the GitHub repository above.
