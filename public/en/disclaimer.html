Disclaimer and Information

Last updated: March 29, 2026.

1. Prayer Times

Times are displayed from the Islamic Association of Sweden's (IFIS) prayer times widget (or equivalent technical intermediary). Small discrepancies may occur depending on the calculation method, updates, or technical errors.

For fasting and religious decisions, you should follow your local congregation or mosque and applicable official announcements.

2. Friday Prayer / Jumu’ah

If the app shows a Friday row, the time is based on the same source as the day's Dhuhr; actual jamaat times may differ between mosques.

3. Ramadan (imsak / iftar)

In Ramadan, fajr and maghrib are shown as practical references for fasting/breaking. Local rules and mosque guidance may vary.

4. Qibla (Compass)

Qibla is shown as a geographical direction from north and does not use the device's compass sensor. Check against a reliable source if necessary.

5. Hijri Calendar

Hijri dates are shown with a calendar that follows common practice in the interface (Umm al-Qura). Local decisions on moon sighting may differ.

6. Location and City

GPS and city names are used to show estimated times. Incorrect positioning or a misspelled city can produce incorrect results.

7. No Professional Advice

The app is an informational tool and does not constitute legal, medical, or religious advice.
