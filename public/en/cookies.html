Cookies and local storage

Last updated: 10 April 2026.

This information supports ePrivacy-related transparency (EU / Swedish practice) about storage in your browser or app.

1. Overview

Call to Prayer Sweden mainly uses local storage on the device — browser localStorage and, in the Android app, Capacitor Preferences — for settings (city, language, theme, adhan, reminders, etc.) and to cache prayer times for temporary offline use.

2. Browser cookies

We do not use our own cookies for marketing or cross-site behavioural tracking. Any technical cookies from the host may follow the provider’s policy if present.

3. Third parties when you choose a feature

If you use features that send requests to external services, they may process connection data under their policy, e.g. OpenStreetMap Nominatim for reverse geocoding when you use GPS. Adhan is played from files bundled in the app and normally does not require cookies for playback.

4. Your choices

You can clear site data in the browser or app data on Android; that removes saved settings and cache. You can also revoke location, notification, and other permissions in system settings.
